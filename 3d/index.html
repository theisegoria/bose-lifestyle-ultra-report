The architectural difference

01 / APPLE

Control around the cabinet.

HomePod combines a woofer, a five-tweeter ring, and microphone-informed processing. Its room sensing adapts playback to nearby surfaces.

02 / BOSE

Control through reflection.

Lifestyle Ultra combines forward sound with an upward radiator. Its separate port contributes to the bass system; clear space above matters to the height path.

Sources & model boundaries

What the model can show.

This companion extends the published technical investigation. Product proportions and visible component arrangements follow public references. Mesh weave, exact curvatures, horn passages, circuit details and duct cross-sections remain approximations.

Room paths are ideal single reflections in a rectangular room, using 343 m/s for the speed of sound. They do not predict loudness, directivity, frequency response or perceived height. A shelf truncates the drawn Bose ray; diffraction is not modelled.

The original report’s patents describe disclosed approaches, not confirmed firmware. Bose’s FCC internal-photo exhibit is listed for release on 11 November 2026; its internal model therefore relies on the public marketing cutaway, not an inspected production unit.
